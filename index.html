04 Selected Projects
↗ VLMs · Industrial CV
VLM Robustness on Industrial Defects

A reproducible study of how vision-language models (CLIP, SigLIP) hold up for industrial defect classification on MVTec-AD under realistic factory corruptions — lighting, blur, sensor noise, compression, occlusion. Zero-shot baseline across 15 categories and 3 prompt strategies, with few-shot LoRA fine-tuning underway. Finding: zero-shot VLMs are weak on industrial defects (best mean balanced accuracy 0.638, AUROC 0.816), and SigLIP beats CLIP by 5–8 points.

Python · PyTorch · CLIP / SigLIP · LoRA

ROS 2 Assistant using LLMs

An agentic natural-language-to-code assistant for ROS 2 (Jazzy) built on the GPT-4 API. Turns free-form commands into ROS 2 nodes, publishers, subscribers, services, clients and timers, and persists each generated node's topology as JSON for context-aware, incremental edits across sessions — a lightweight tool-use / memory pattern.
